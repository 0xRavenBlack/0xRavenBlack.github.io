Time flows like shadows. Let us help you keep up. ✨

Are you tired of sterile, soulless scheduling apps? Step into the velvet embrace of
**ShadowDate** — a native **Rust + GTK4** desktop calendar with a moody, dark-pastel
soul. Designed for those who find beauty in the shadows, ShadowDate keeps your life
elegantly organized in a single, seamless iCalendar (`.ics`) file. What you see is
exactly what you share. No bloat. No tracking. Just pure, aesthetic harmony.

![ShadowDate]({{ '/assets/img/posts/shadowdate-1.jpg' | relative_url }})

**🖤 The Aesthetic:**

A soothing palette of Lavender, Mint, Peach, and Lilac resting on deep Charcoal. A
translucent, gothic muse watches softly over your schedule from the backdrop, turning
every glance at your calendar into a moment of dark tranquility.

**🗓️ The Experience:**

A Solid 6×7 Month Grid with compact, beautiful color-dot indicators for every day.
Keyboard-First Navigation: Glide through your month with arrow keys. Enter to create.
Fluid, effortless, intimate. Multilingual Soul: fluent in 7 languages
(🇬🇧 🇩🇪 🇫🇷 🇪🇸 🇨🇳 🇯🇵 🇵🇱), auto-detected to speak directly to you. A Silent Guardian
Daemon: a headless systemd service that whispers desktop notifications right when you
need them, ensuring you never miss a moment.

**⚙️ Built for the Modern Linux Desktop:**

Perfectly tailored for Wayland and Hyprland (with custom floating window rules),
ShadowDate is fixed at a gorgeous 1024 × 560, hovering like a phantom over your
workspace.
