## ✨ Why It Glows

GRIDLOCK is a love letter to the classic **1010!** formula, rebuilt from scratch with a smooth neon/glossy identity. The wild part? It ships with **no `.png`, no `.wav`, and no font files at all** — every pixel is drawn from code and every sound is synthesized as 16-bit PCM at startup. 🎛️

- **Smooth neon / glossy UI** — built entirely from theme style boxes and a custom animated background shader, no bitmap art required.
- **"Almost" line hints** — any row or column one cell short of clearing pulses the board frame and pins the exact missing cell, so you can plan the chain.
- **Juicy clears** — a light beam sweeps each cleared line, tiles flash and pop, sparks burst, the screen shakes, the score floats, and tiered **DOUBLE / TRIPLE / MEGA** banners celebrate the damage.
- **Chain the lights** — clear on consecutive moves to grow a streak; a single clear can cascade into multi-line bonuses.
- **Procedural audio** — generated tones *and* a soft chill-out music bed (a warm detuned pad, sub-bass and a drifting bell melody). Zero binary audio assets.
- **Lifetime stats** — games played, lines cleared, blocks placed and best streak persist and appear on the game-over card.
- **Full-screen by default** with a persistent toggle (`F11`), plus separate sound and music volume.
- **Deterministic, fully tested rules** with a weighted shuffle bag so piece droughts are rare.

## 🎯 How to Play

1. Pick up a piece from the tray at the bottom.
2. Drop it on empty cells. Glowing previews show whether the placement is legal (cyan = valid, red = invalid).
3. Fill an entire row or column to clear it and score. A pulsing frame and a pinned dot reveal lines that are **one cell** from clearing.
4. Clear multiple lines at once for a multiline bonus; clear on consecutive moves for a streak bonus.
5. The game ends when no remaining piece can be placed — *gridlock*.

## 🏆 Scoring

| Rule | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Points per line | `100` |
| Multiline bonus | `+0.5×` per extra line |
| Streak bonus | `+0.25×` per consecutive clearing move |

```
score = 100 × lines × (1 + 0.5 × (lines − 1)) × (1 + 0.25 × (streak − 1))
```

A move that clears nothing resets the streak. Simple to learn, brutal to master. 💜

## 🏗️ Built the Hard Way

Under the neon there's a strict separation between **rules** and **presentation** — the single most important convention in the repo:

- **`GameRules`** owns the grid, tray, scoring, near-complete hints and game-over detection. It's pure logic — no nodes, no input, no rendering — which means it's fully unit-testable headlessly.
- **`Game`** (autoload) holds the current rules plus persisted best score and lifetime stats.
- The **scene controller** reads input, calls the rules, and renders the returned result (beams, banners, sparks, hints). It holds no rules of its own.

That discipline pays off in the test suite: **105** assertions over the core rules plus **23** headless integration checks that boot the real game and drive a full drag from tray to board. **128 checks, all passing.** ✅
